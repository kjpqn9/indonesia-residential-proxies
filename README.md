## FAQ: Residential Proxy Indonesia

**Q: What's the difference between a residential proxy and a datacenter proxy for Indonesia?**
A residential proxy uses an IP from a real Indonesian ISP assigned to a home or mobile device. A datacenter proxy uses an IP from a hosting provider. Indonesian e-commerce, banking, and major social platforms detect and block datacenter IPs aggressively. Residential IPs blend in because they look identical to ordinary user traffic.

**Q: Are residential proxies legal in Indonesia?**
Using residential proxies for legitimate purposes—market research, SEO tracking, ad verification, brand protection—is legal under Indonesian law. What matters is what you do with them. Scraping public data within site terms of service: fine. Bypassing authentication, scraping personal data, or violating computer access laws: not fine. Always check the target site's robots.txt and terms.

**Q: How fast are Indonesian residential proxies?**
Sped depends on the underlying connection. Residential IPs from urban fiber connections in Jakarta or Surabaya typically deliver 20–80 Mbps. Mobile residential IPs from 4G connections vary more—anywhere from 5 to 30 Mbps. Rural connections are slower. For most scraping and ad verification work, this is fine.

**Q: Can I get a sticky Indonesian IP that lasts longer than 30 minutes?**
Webshare's standard sticky sessions hold for up to 30 minutes. For longer sessions, you can either rotate sticky sessions back to back (auto-renewing) or use IP whitelisting.
